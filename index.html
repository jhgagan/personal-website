Projects

A selection of my academic and personal projects spanning wireless simulation, edge AI, mobile app development, and graphics programming.

Semantic Communication Simulation with Sionna

My Master's Thesis project focused on simulating semantic communication via Feature Identification with Sionna. I designed a modular multi-process architecture using gRPC for interprocess communication and utilized Nvidia's Sionna library for physical-layer wireless simulation.

Trenord App with GeoFencing Features

Developed a mobile application implementing geo-fencing to provide location-based notifications, targeted advertisements, and accurate ETA predictions for users.

Bluetooth-LE 6.0 Distance Estimation

Evaluated BLE 6.0 distance estimation techniques (RSSI, PBR, RTT) against UWB (DS-TWR) technologies. This project utilized TinyML on data collected from a Nordic nRF54L15 Dev Kit for edge AI applications.

Modular Hospital Ward Planner

Developed a modular hospital ward planner utilizing the Vulkan framework. The project involved implementing vertex and fragment shaders, allowing for asset placement and rotation across multiple camera views.

Publications

My research contributions have led to multiple publications in international journals and conferences, primarily focusing on automated medical image segmentation.

Cháksu: A Glaucoma-specific Fundus Image Database

Published in Scientific Data - Nature. Involved the acquisition of fundus images, generation of binary maps from expert annotations, data analysis, and visualization across multiple parameters.

Automated Optic Disc Segmentation Using Basis-Splines-based Active Contour

Published in IEEE Access. The segmentation model was validated on Drishti-GS, Messidor, RIGA, and a local dataset, achieving high Dice scores (up to 0.9343).

Automated Segmentation of Common Carotid Artery in Ultrasound Images

Published in IEEE Access. Achieved Dice indices of 0.9332 (lumen-intima) and 0.9175 (media-adventitia), successfully outperforming previous results by 0.0439.

A Unified Approach for Automated Segmentation of Pupil and Iris in On-axis Images

Published in Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine. The segmentation approach was successfully validated on the IIT Delhi Iris and CASIA V3/V4 datasets.

RaPiD: A Raspberry Pi-based Optical Fundoscope

Published in the ISSS Journal of Micro and Smart Systems. Developed and tested an affordable Raspberry Pi-based fundus imaging device on a specimen human eye.

Automatic Segmentation of Fovea and Macula in Retinal Fundus Images

Presented at IEEE Region 10 (TENCON) 2022. Utilized elliptical active contour methods on 508 retinal fundus images, achieving a 99.66% fovea localization accuracy.

Automatic Optic Cup Segmentation Using Affine Snakes in Gradient Vector Field

Presented at IEEE INDICON 2023. Proposed an affine-snake model driven by gradient vector flow for automated optic cup segmentation in fundus images.

Segmentation of Left Ventricle in Cardiac MR Images

Available on ArXiv. Implemented multiscale template matching and elliptical active contours for LV segmentation, validated on 320 scans from the M&Ms Challenge.
